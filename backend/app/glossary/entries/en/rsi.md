**RSI** is a 14-day momentum oscillator that quantifies the size of
recent gains relative to recent losses.

### How to read it

- **50** — neutral.
- **> 70** — often called "overbought" — recent gains have been outsized;
  short-term pullbacks are common.
- **< 30** — "oversold" — recent losses have been outsized; short-term
  bounces are common.
- **50–70** — strong but not exhausted (often a healthy trend).

### Cautions

- RSI is a *short-term* indicator. A stock can stay overbought (> 70)
  for weeks during a strong trend — selling purely on RSI > 70 cuts you
  out early.
- It's most useful as **context**, not as a standalone signal. We
  combine it with 12-1 momentum and the SMA50/200 cross in the momentum
  strategy.
