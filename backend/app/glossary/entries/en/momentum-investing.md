**Momentum** is the persistent tendency of recently-winning stocks to
keep winning (and losers to keep losing) over the medium term —
typically **3 to 12 months**.

### The signals we use

1. **12-1 momentum.** Total return over the last 12 months, excluding the
   most recent month. This is the canonical academic momentum factor.
2. **RSI(14).** Strong (50–70) is healthy; > 80 is overbought; < 30 is
   oversold.
3. **SMA50 vs. SMA200.** When the 50-day average crosses above the 200-day
   ("golden cross"), trend is up. The opposite is a "death cross".

### When momentum works

- Steady trends with broad participation (e.g. a multi-year tech
  expansion).
- Less effective in choppy sideways markets — that's where momentum
  strategies are most prone to whipsaws.

### Risks

- Momentum reverses fast at major market turns — exiting after the
  reversal often means giving back most of the gain.
- Concentration risk: momentum portfolios end up overweight whichever
  sector has been winning, which can be brutal when the leadership
  rotates.

This advisor uses momentum **as one signal among several**, weighted up
or down by your risk tolerance. Aggressive profiles lean on it harder;
conservative profiles use it more as confirmation than as the lead.
