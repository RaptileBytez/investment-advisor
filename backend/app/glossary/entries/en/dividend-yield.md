---
key: dividend-yield
title: Dividend yield
short: Annual dividends as a percentage of the share price.
related: [value-investing, price-to-earnings]
---

**Dividend yield** = annual dividends per share ÷ current share price.

A yield of **3 %** means: if the dividend rate holds steady, every €100
you invest pays out €3 per year in cash.

### How to read it

| Yield     | Notes                                                        |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0 %       | Many growth names reinvest 100 % of earnings.                |
| 1 – 3 %   | Typical large-cap.                                           |
| 3 – 6 %   | Income-focused; mature businesses, REITs, utilities.         |
| > 6 %     | Often a warning — high yield can mean the price has fallen.  |
| > 10 %    | "Value trap" risk — verify the dividend is actually paid.    |

### What it doesn't tell you

- **Sustainability.** A high yield from a company with shrinking earnings
  often gets cut.
- **Total return.** A 1 % yield with 12 % price growth beats a 6 % yield
  with no growth.
