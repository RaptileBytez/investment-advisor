**Buy & Hold** is the simplest investment strategy: buy a quality asset
and hold it for years or decades, ignoring short-term fluctuations.

### Why it works

- **Compounding.** Long holding periods let returns compound. Skipping
  even the best 10 days in a decade can roughly halve a portfolio's
  return.
- **Low costs.** Few trades → low fees, low taxes, low effort.
- **Behavioural advantage.** Trying to time the market is the largest
  source of underperformance for retail investors.

### What this advisor looks for

We score Buy & Hold candidates by:
- Long-term **CAGR** (do they actually compound?),
- **Annualised volatility** (can you actually hold through it?),
- **Max drawdown** (penalty for punishing past losses).

### What it requires from you

- A horizon of **5+ years** ideally; ten is better.
- The discipline to hold through a -30 % or worse drawdown without
  panic-selling. If you can't, this strategy isn't for you on its own —
  combine with DCA to soften entry timing.
